    Know the pieces

    Select a piece to hear how it moves and feel its vibration. Short buzzes are the smaller pieces, long buzzes the bigger ones.

    Chess words
    File
    A column of squares, named a to h.
    Rank
    A row of squares, numbered 1 to 8.
    Capture
    Moving onto an enemy piece, which removes it from the board.
    Check
    The king is under attack and must be saved right away.
    Checkmate
    The king is in check with no way out. The game is over and the attacker wins.
    Stalemate
    A player is not in check but has no legal move. The game is a draw.
    Castling
    The king moves two squares toward a rook, and the rook jumps to its other side.
    Promotion
    A pawn that reaches the far side becomes a queen, rook, bishop or knight.
    En passant
    A pawn captures an enemy pawn that has just moved two squares past it.
    Hanging piece
    A piece nobody guards. It can be taken for free.
    Fork
    One piece attacks two enemy pieces at once.
    Skewer
    An attack on a big piece that has a smaller one behind it on the same line.
    How Touchpoint works

    Every square is a labelled control, so VoiceOver and TalkBack read it aloud as "e4, white pawn", and refreshable braille displays show the same text. Deaf players get a caption for every move and a visual alert for check. Deafblind players can type moves from a braille keyboard, and on Android they can feel the board by vibration. Nothing relies on colour alone: selected, last-move and check squares each have their own pattern.

    If the app's speech does not work
    • Using a screen reader? You do not need the app's speech. VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA and JAWS read everything already. Leave App speech off so you do not hear things twice.
    • Turn it on. Use the Speech button at the top. It starts off each visit, and only talks after you press a key or tap.
    • Check the volume. Turn up the device volume, and on an iPhone check the ring and silent switch: silent mode mutes web speech. Check that headphones or a Bluetooth speaker are connected, or unplugged.
    • Try another voice. Choose a different Voice in Speech and sound above, then use Test voice. On Windows and Android, voices may need to be downloaded in the device's own speech settings.
    • Try another browser. Some browsers have no voices. Chrome, Edge and Safari usually do.
    • Still nothing? Every message is also written in the message bar at the top and in Captions on the Play tab, so you can keep playing.
