    Learn

    Short lessons on the real board, from your first square to your first game. Take as long as you like: nothing moves on until you say so.

    Know the pieces

    Each piece has its own vibration. Select one to feel it and hear how it moves. Short buzzes are the smaller pieces, long buzzes are the bigger ones.

    Chess words

    File
    A column of squares, named a to h.
    Rank
    A row of squares, numbered 1 to 8.
    Capture
    Moving onto an enemy piece, which removes it from the board.
    Check
    The king is under attack and must be saved right away.
    Checkmate
    The king is in check with no way out. The game is over and the attacker wins.
    Stalemate
    A player is not in check but has no legal move. The game is a draw.
    Castling
    The king moves two squares toward a rook, and the rook jumps to its other side.
    Promotion
    A pawn that reaches the far side becomes a queen, rook, bishop or knight.
    En passant
    A pawn captures an enemy pawn that has just moved two squares past it.

    How Touchpoint works

    Every square is a labelled control, so VoiceOver and TalkBack read it aloud as "e4, white pawn", and refreshable braille displays show the same text. Deaf players get a caption for every move and a visual alert for check. Deafblind players can type moves from a braille keyboard, and on Android they can feel the board by vibration. Nothing relies on colour alone: selected, last-move and check squares each have their own pattern.
