    Learn

    Short lessons that run on the real board. Every goal is spoken, captioned and felt, so you can learn by ear, by touch or by sight.

    Feel the pieces

    Each piece has its own vibration. Tap one to feel it and hear its name. Short buzzes are the minor pieces, long buzzes are the major ones.

    How Touchpoint works

    Every square is a labelled control, so VoiceOver and TalkBack read it aloud as "e4, white pawn". Refreshable braille displays show the same text. Deaf players get a caption for every move and a colour-and-pattern alert for check. Deafblind players can explore by vibration and type moves from a braille keyboard. Nothing relies on colour alone: selected, last-move and check squares each have their own pattern.
